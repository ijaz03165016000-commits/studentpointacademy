About Us

Where Every Child Learns & Grows

Student Point Academy is an educational platform designed to support children from early learning to school-level academic excellence. We provide subject-wise tuition, digital education, creative learning, basic computer skills, handwriting improvement, Quran learning, kids' activities, and parent-friendly progress support.

Our mission is to make learning easy, smart, affordable, and result-oriented for every child.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers. Feel free to contact us if you need more information.

What age group do you teach?
We teach students from age 4 to 18 years. We have specially designed programs for each age group — from early childhood learning to Matric (9–10) and Intermediate (FSc / ICS) exam preparation.
Do you offer free demo classes?
Yes! Parents can book a free demo class before admission. This allows you to understand our teaching method, meet the teacher, and see if the program fits your child's needs — with no obligation whatsoever.
Can students choose individual subjects?
Absolutely. Subject-wise tuition is fully available. Students can enroll for just one subject or multiple subjects based on their specific needs and budget.
Do you offer online classes?
Yes! We offer both online and offline (physical) classes. Parents can choose the mode that is most convenient for their family. Online classes are available via WhatsApp, Zoom, or Google Meet.
Do parents receive progress updates?
Yes. Parents receive regular updates about their child's performance including weekly progress reports, test results, homework status, and teacher feedback. Parent consultations are also available on request.
Can I select my preferred time slot?
Yes! We offer four one-hour slots: 4–5 PM, 5–6 PM, 6–7 PM and 7–8 PM (Monday to Saturday), online or physical. You can choose the slot that best matches your child's school timing and daily routine.
How can I pay the fee online?
Send the fee via Easypaisa to 0344-0807888 (Account title: Muhammad Ijaz). Then upload the receipt in the payment form on this website or send the screenshot on WhatsApp at 0344-0807888.
Do you teach Cambridge / O Level students?
Yes. Along with the National curriculum (AJK, Federal and Punjab boards) we support International curriculum students — Cambridge Primary, O Level, IGCSE and A Level subjects — online and physically.
How long are the Arduino and IoT courses?
Each course runs for 3 months with 2 classes per week. The fee is Rs. 15,000 for the complete course, including hands-on projects.
How can I contact Student Point Academy?
You can reach us on WhatsApp at 0344-0807888, visit us at G1/Part 3, near MedBros Pharmacy, Haul Road, Mirpur AJK, fill in the demo class form, or visit our website www.studentpointacademy.online.
