    Questions

    Do I need a laptop?

    No. A phone with internet is enough — take photos of homework and record voice notes in the browser.

    How are Duas and Qirat taught?

    The teacher posts the Dua with Arabic text and records it in their own voice. The student listens, practises and sends a recording back. The teacher replies with corrections — in writing or by voice.

    Which classes can join?

    Play Group to Intermediate. Each student sees only their own class's work.

    What is the difference between Silver, Gold and Diamond?

    The main difference is daily one-to-one time with the teacher: 30 minutes (Silver), 40 minutes (Gold) or 1 hour with individual consultancy (Diamond). Higher packages also get faster checking, voice feedback and progress reports.

    Can I try before paying?

    Yes — a free demo class for 1 day. Message us on WhatsApp to book it.

    How do I renew?

    From the student portal → Subscription. Pay by NayaPay, SadaPay, Easypaisa or JazzCash and enter the Transaction ID. Students keep the price they joined with.
